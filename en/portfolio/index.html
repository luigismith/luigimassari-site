  • 2 photos Instruments Eremo The synthesizer that plays by itself and never hits a wrong note.

    Switch it on and it starts: a soundscape that slowly changes and always stays consonant. Ten atmospheres, from Night to Forest, a five-voice drone, tape echo and reverb; on the round display the wave moves like on an oscilloscope.

    Inside there is a Teensy 4.1 with firmware written for it. Outside, a laser-engraved plywood panel with gothic lettering and a 3D printed box: four knobs and two keys are all it takes to play it.

  • 4 photos Instruments Open Ichosynth A groovebox to build with your own hands, born for a workshop.

    Sampler, synthesizer and step sequencer on a 256-LED grid: every row is a voice, every column a step. It is the TŒRN groovebox by SP_ rebuilt with common parts, with no custom circuit boards.

    It was born for ICHOS 2026, in Taranto: every participant soldered and assembled their own. That is why it has manuals in Italian and English, a wiring guide drawn part by part, a case that prints in three pieces and an emulator to try it before soldering.

  • 4 photos Instruments Open Stame One key, a full chord: the instrument that lets harmony play.

    One hand picks the chord type, the other the note: Stame adds bass, rhythm and, if you like, an ambient bed that almost plays itself. 232 sounds, 43 drum kits, a looper, all written in C for a small handheld console.

    Keyboard, key pad and console sit in a single case, designed to measure and 3D printed in three colours, in trays that fit together without glue. Software, manual and case design are public.

  • Instruments EtherVox Armsonic A granular theremin: you play the sound with your hands in the air.

    Like a theremin, it is played without touching it; but instead of the classic whistle there is granular synthesis, which breaks the sound into grains and puts them back together. Knobs set frequency, sync and decay; a screen shows the scale in use, such as the pentatonic.

    The front is laser-engraved wood, with the name and legends drawn for it; the two side blocks are 3D printed.

  • Instruments Mutant Synthesizer A synthesizer you breed: species, population, mutation.

    At the centre there is a matrix of 64 LEDs; around it, knobs that are not called «filter» or «volume» but species, population, lifespan, mutation. The sound is born, grows and changes like a small ecosystem.

    The panel is plywood, laser cut and engraved, with a pattern of holes echoing the LEDs; the sloped case is 3D printed.

  • 2 photos Laser Arcade cabinet An arcade in the living room, built from scratch.

    A full-size cabinet with a Back to the Future theme: a bartop with screen, two joysticks and lit buttons, on a stand with S-shaped sides. The control panel echoes the DeLorean's time circuits.

    It is a job that brings the whole bench together: the laser-cut cabinet, the artwork designed and printed, the software that launches the games, the electrical and electronic work for buttons and lights, and 3D printed parts where needed.

  • Software and games Ctrl-Alt-Revenge Taranto, 2087: a video game of puppets and cardboard.

    A 2.5D action platformer: you run, jump and climb over a city built like a diorama, with characters that move in steps like stop-motion puppets. The hero is GIG, fifty, whose arm has been switched off.

  • Software and games Open Cirmolo A game console that becomes a musical instrument, in Italian.

    A system for the Miyoo Flip handheld: an interface entirely in Italian, synthesizers and samplers to play with a MIDI keyboard, and an AI that translates games and suggests what to play.

    It is a fork of spruceOS; some of the fixes made for Cirmolo were accepted into the original project.

  • Software and games Open GB-Tracker Pro Composing music on a Game Boy Color, note by note.

    A four-channel music tracker that runs on a real Game Boy Color: you write notes in a grid and the console plays them with its original chip.

    It is written in assembly, the language closest to the machine, and includes a chiptune version of Für Elise.
