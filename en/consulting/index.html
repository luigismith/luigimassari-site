Proposals

AI assistant on documents

An assistant that answers questions by searching contracts, manuals and procedures, and always shows where the answer comes from. Documents stay where they are.

For firms, offices and businesses with archives where information is hard to find.

Automating a repetitive job

A task done by hand across emails, PDFs and spreadsheets becomes automatic. It starts with a single procedure, and the time saved is measured.

For those who repeat the same operations across different programs every day.

IT check-up

Network, backups, accounts, security and licences are reviewed. The result is an ordered list: what to fix now, what can wait.

For those without in-house IT staff, or who want a second opinion.

Custom software

Business software, web applications and websites built around the way their users work, from analysis to going live. The code is handed over.

For those running a business on spreadsheets, emails and scattered documents.
