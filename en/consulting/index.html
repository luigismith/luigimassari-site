Proposals

AI assistant on documents

It answers questions by searching contracts, manuals and procedures, and every answer shows its document and page. If your data must not leave, it runs on a computer on your premises. The archive can also be built from scratch, with notes and emails in plain files.

For firms, offices and businesses with archives where information is hard to find.

Automating a repetitive job

A task done by hand across emails, PDFs and spreadsheets becomes automatic. It starts with a single procedure, and the time saved is measured.

For those who repeat the same operations across different programs every day.

IT check-up

Network, backups, accounts, security and licences are reviewed. The result is an ordered list: what to fix now, what can wait.

For those without in-house IT staff, or who want a second opinion.

Custom software

Business software and web applications built around the way their users work, from analysis to going live. The code is handed over.

For those keeping customers, orders or deadlines in spreadsheets that have grown too big.

Websites

Showcase sites, portfolios and catalogues, also multilingual and with 3D parts like this one. Light pages, with text and structured data written for search.

For professionals, businesses and associations starting from scratch or replacing an old site.
