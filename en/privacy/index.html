Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Data controller

Luigi Massari, Taranto (TA), Italia. Email: info@luigimassari.com.

What data I process and why

Browsing. The site is hosted by OVHcloud, which records technical server logs such as IP address, date and requested page, for security and service operation (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are kept for the period set by the provider.

Contact. If you write to me, I process your name, email and message only to reply and, if asked, prepare a quote (pre-contractual steps, Art. 6(1)(b)). The site's form stores nothing: it opens your email app.

Purchases. Payments happen on Stripe, which processes payment data as an independent controller; I receive your name, email, shipping address and order details to fulfil the order and meet tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(c)). I never see or store card details. Order data is kept for 10 years for accounting purposes.

Cookies

This site uses no profiling cookies and no analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are hosted on the site itself, with no calls to external services. At checkout, Stripe may set technical cookies required for payment security.

Who receives the data

OVHcloud (hosting, EU) and Stripe (payments) as providers. For shipping, the chosen courier receives name, address and contact details. No data is sold or shared for marketing.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing by writing to info@luigimassari.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or your local authority.
