Products

Elettrofoni are hand-built electronic instruments, sold assembled or as kits. Photos and illustrations are indicative: small differences in finish are typical of handmade work. For pre-order products, the product page states which features ship at delivery and which arrive through later updates.

Prices and payment

Prices are in euros. The total, including any taxes and shipping, is shown on the payment page before you confirm. Payment is handled by Stripe by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The contract is concluded when you receive the payment confirmation email.

Delivery

Made-to-order and pre-order instruments are built after purchase: estimated times are shown on the product page or emailed within two working days of your order. Shipping is tracked. If the delivery time can't be met, you can cancel and get a full refund.

Right of withdrawal

If you buy as a consumer you have 14 days from delivery to withdraw without giving a reason (Italian Consumer Code, Art. 52 ff., implementing EU Directive 2011/83). Email info@luigimassari.com with your order, then return the product intact within 14 days. Return shipping is at your expense. The refund, including standard initial shipping, is issued within 14 days to the original payment method. Withdrawal does not apply to products made to your personal specifications (Art. 59(c)).

Warranty

Consumers are entitled to the 24-month legal guarantee of conformity from delivery. For kits, the warranty covers the supplied components but not damage caused during assembly. If something's wrong, write to me and we'll agree on repair, replacement or refund.

Governing law

Italian law applies. Consumers keep the right to bring proceedings in the courts of their place of residence.
