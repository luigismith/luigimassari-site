3D modelling, printing and laser

From idea to an object in your hand.

Custom 3D modelling, 3D printing, laser cutting and engraving: starting from a drawing, a sketch or a part to copy, ending with an object you can try for real.

Contact me for more information

  • Prototypes you can touch

    The first printed part shows whether the idea holds up: try it, fix the model, print again.

  • Enclosures for electronics

    Custom printed cases and mounts for boards, instruments and devices, with laser-cut and engraved front panels, like those of the Elettrofoni.

  • Laser cutting and engraving

    Panels, engraved lettering and graphics, jigs, lamps and made-to-drawing objects, cut from sheet materials.

  • Spare parts and small batches

    The knob, the hatch, the clip that is no longer made: measured, redrawn, remade. And several identical parts, with no moulds and no minimum order.
