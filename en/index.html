3D and laser

Custom prototypes and parts.

3D modelling, 3D printing, laser cutting and engraving: it starts from a drawing, a sketch or a part to copy, and ends with an object to try for real.

Contact me for more information

  • From drawing to part

    A sketch, a photograph or a description becomes a 3D model and then a printed part: prototypes, enclosures, mounts, accessories. The part is tried and, if needed, corrected.

  • The spare part that can't be found

    A broken or discontinued part is measured, redrawn and remade: knobs, hooks, hatches, gears, clips.

  • Laser cut and engraved

    Plaques, signs, panels, finger-joint boxes and objects with engraved lettering and drawings, starting from a file or an idea. On wood and plywood, acrylic, cardboard, leather and fabrics.

  • Small personalised batches

    Dozens of identical parts, or each with its own name: gadgets, key rings, place cards, displays.

Training

Hands-on training: learning by doing.

Courses for schools, institutions, associations and workplaces: few slides, lots of practice. Each format is a reference and is adapted to the organiser.

Contact me for more information

  • Building to learn

    3D printing, electronics and sound: a small instrument is assembled or an object is printed, and how it works becomes clear. Reference format: a course of four sessions.

  • IT security basics

    Passwords, email scams, backups, phone and computer in order. For non-technical people. Reference format: half a day.

  • AI at school

    For teachers and students: how it works, how to use it for studying and preparing lessons, where it gets things wrong, how to use it responsibly. Reference format: a course of three sessions.

  • AI in the office

    For people who work with documents, emails and spreadsheets: AI assistants applied to everyday tasks, with clear rules on data and confidentiality. Reference format: a full day.

IT and AI

Bring AI into your business.

AI assistants, automation, infrastructure reviews and custom software. Each job starts from how the work is done today and is defined in writing before it begins; code and data stay with whoever commissioned them.

Contact me for more informationAll proposals

  • AI assistant on documents

    An assistant that answers questions by searching contracts, manuals and procedures, and always shows where the answer comes from. Documents stay where they are.

  • Automating a repetitive job

    A task done by hand across emails, PDFs and spreadsheets becomes automatic. It starts with a single procedure, and the time saved is measured.

  • IT check-up

    Network, backups, accounts, security and licences are reviewed. The result is an ordered list: what to fix now, what can wait.

Profile

Portfolio

Luigi Massari has worked in IT since 2001: systems, networks, security and software for businesses. In Taranto, Luigi designs custom software, builds electronic instruments and teaches people to use technology, artificial intelligence included.

There is a single bench. On the same day an AI automation gets written, a part gets designed for 3D printing, a panel gets laser engraved and a synthesizer gets soldered: what is offered is what gets done every day, and personal projects are the testing ground for work done for others.

The philosophy is Open: personal projects are public, with code, schematics, print files and manuals, and what is built for others belongs to whoever commissioned it.

Artificial intelligence is a workshop tool: it helps write code, documentation and images faster and better, with decisions and final checks always in human hands.

For ICHOS 2026, a sound ecology workshop in Taranto, Luigi designed Ichosynth: the groovebox every participant soldered and assembled with their own hands.

Open

Open by choice.

Open means nothing stays locked in a drawer: personal projects are public, to study and to rebuild, and what is built for others belongs to whoever commissioned it and keeps working even without the person who made it.

  • Code and files handed over

    Source code and project files are delivered together with the work.

  • No lock-in

    No closed platforms or forced subscriptions: what is built keeps working even without the person who made it.

  • Open tools

    Where a good open alternative exists, that is the one used.

  • Public projects

    Personal projects are published with code, schematics and print files.
