3D and laser

Custom prototypes and parts.

3D modelling, 3D printing, laser cutting and engraving: it starts from a drawing, a sketch or a part to copy, and ends with an object to try for real.

Contact me for more information

  • From drawing to part

    Prototypes, enclosures, mounts and accessories. The 3D model starts from measurements, the part is printed, you try it in your hand and, if needed, it gets corrected; then as many copies as you need.

  • The spare part that can't be found

    A broken or discontinued part is measured, redrawn and remade: knobs, hooks, hatches, gears, clips. If the part can't be shipped, photos with a tape measure next to it are enough.

  • Laser cut and engraved

    Plates, signs, panels, finger-joint boxes and personalisation, starting from a file or an idea. On wood and plywood, black acrylic, cardboard, leather and fabric.

  • Panels and enclosures for electronics

    For a circuit you have already built: a laser-engraved front panel with the control legends and a 3D printed box, drawn on the measurements of the parts. In wood or acrylic, not metal.

Profile

Portfolio

Luigi Massari works in Taranto across 3D printing, laser, electronics, training and software. He has worked on systems, networks and IT security since 2001, and has been 3D printing and laser cutting since 2012.

There is a single bench: on the same day an automation gets written, a part gets designed for printing, a panel gets engraved and a synthesizer gets soldered. Personal projects are the test run for the work done for others.

He works on sound away from the bench too: sound walks and field recordings in the city's marginal places, which then find their way into the courses and the instruments.

Artificial intelligence is a workshop tool: it helps write code, manuals and images, while decisions and final checks stay with a person. Images made or retouched with AI are always labelled.

For ICHOS 2026, a sound ecology workshop in Taranto, Luigi designed Ichosynth: the groovebox every participant soldered and assembled with their own hands.

Open

Open by choice.

Open means two things: personal projects are public, and what you commission is yours, files included.
