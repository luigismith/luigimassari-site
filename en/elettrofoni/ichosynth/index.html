Ichosynth is the TŒRN groovebox by SP_ (soundpauli) rebuilt with common parts, to be soldered by hand: sampler, synthesizer and step sequencer on a Teensy 4.1. It runs the real, unmodified TŒRN firmware, so every one of its features is there.

How it plays

On the 256-LED RGB grid every row is a voice and every column a step: light a step and it plays. There are eight sample voices and three synth voices, polyphonic, each with filter, reverb, bitcrusher, detune and octave; the synth voices also have a Moog-style ladder filter. Every step has its own velocity, probability and conditions. Patterns have pages and sub-patterns, and song mode chains them.

Samples and recording

Sounds live on a micro-SD card in packs, with a browser to pick them and controls for start point, length and reverse. The 6.35 mm line input records live into the sampler, with an optional count-in: a field recorder, an instrument, another machine. The card ships with a public-domain sample set, and a dedicated tool converts any WAV file.

What changes from TŒRN

The expensive parts of the original become common ones: four KY-040 encoders instead of the RGB encoders, the PLAY, MENU and REC keys instead of capacitive pads, and an OLED screen showing channel, mode, transport, tempo, volume and page. Wiring is point to point, with no custom circuit boards. The case prints in three parts on two plates, without supports, and closes with eight screws; the firmware flashes in one click.

Ready or as a kit

Ichosynth is for sale assembled and tested, or as a kit. For those who build it, the public repository holds the build and user manuals in Italian and English, the illustrated wiring guide, a picture-only assembly guide, ready print projects and an emulator to try it on a computer before soldering.

It is the instrument the participants built with their own hands at ICHOS 2026, a residential sound ecology workshop conceived and led by Francesco Giannico (Taranto, 12–14 June 2026).

The firmware is TŒRN, written by SP_: the credit remains his. Build, case and documentation of Ichosynth are by Luigi Massari.

Specifications

Platform
Teensy 4.1 with audio board and 16 MB of PSRAM
Voices
8 sample voices and 3 synth voices, polyphonic, with filter, reverb and bitcrusher; ladder filter on the synth voices
Sequencer
16×16 RGB grid: one row per voice, one column per step; velocity, probability and conditions per step; pages, sub-patterns and song mode
Samples
Packs on micro-SD, with browser, start point, length and reverse; live recording with count-in
Controls
4 knobs, 3 keys (PLAY, MENU, REC), OLED screen
Inputs and outputs
6.35 mm line input, stereo headphone output, USB; optional DIN MIDI (clock and notes)
Case
3D printed in three parts, without supports, closed by eight screws
Options
Ready to play, or as a kit with the instructions on GitHub
Documentation
Build and user manuals in Italian and English, wiring guide, assembly guide, emulator, workshop material
