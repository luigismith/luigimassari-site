Ichosynth

The TŒRN groovebox rebuilt by hand: a 256-LED grid, four knobs, three keys.

Contact me for more information

Project on GitHub: manuals, print files, emulator ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Ichosynth is the TŒRN groovebox by SP_ (soundpauli) rebuilt with common parts, to be soldered by hand: sampler, synthesizer and step sequencer on a Teensy 4.1. On the 256-LED grid every row is a voice and every column a step.

It is an open project. The public repository holds the build and user manuals in Italian and English, the wiring guide, the files to print the case and an emulator to try it on a computer. No custom circuit boards are needed.

It is the instrument the participants built with their own hands at ICHOS 2026, a residential sound ecology workshop conceived and led by Francesco Giannico (Taranto, 12–14 June 2026).

The firmware is TŒRN, written by SP_: the credit remains his. Build, case and documentation of Ichosynth are by Luigi Massari.

Specifications

Platform
Teensy 4.1 with audio board
Voices
8 sample voices and 3 synth voices, with filter, reverb and bitcrusher
Sequencer
16×16 RGB grid: one row per voice, one column per step; pages and song mode
Controls
4 knobs, 3 keys (PLAY, MENU, REC), OLED screen
Inputs and outputs
6.35 mm line input for recording, headphone output, optional MIDI DIN
Case
3D printed in three parts, closed by eight screws
Firmware
TŒRN by SP_ (soundpauli), unmodified
Licence
MIT; TŒRN and the libraries keep their own

Handmade

Assembled and tested one by one before shipping.

Warranty and returns

24-month legal warranty and a 14-day right of withdrawal under EU consumer law.
