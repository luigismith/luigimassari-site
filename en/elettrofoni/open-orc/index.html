Pre-order Ask about it

Tracked shipping. Delivery times confirmed when you order.

Open-Orc turns a MIDI keyboard into a harmonic instrument: pick a key and a voicing, press a key and get a full chord with a bass line. It plugs straight into a USB controller keyboard, no computer needed.

The instrument grows in stages: three synth engines, voicing control, key modes, bass engine, looper, drum machine and effects. Pre-order it and you get the version available at delivery plus later firmware updates.

Specifications

Processor
Teensy 4.1, ARM Cortex-M7 at 600 MHz
Audio
Teensy Audio Shield, SGTL5000 codec
Interface
0.96" OLED display and push encoder
Control
