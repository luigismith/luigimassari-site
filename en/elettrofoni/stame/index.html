Stame brings together in one case a keyboard, a pad of mechanical keys and a handheld console that acts as screen and sound engine: press one key and a full chord plays, with bass and rhythm.

How it plays

One hand holds a pad: the chord type on top (Dim, Min, Maj, Sus), the extensions below (6, m7, M7, 9). The other picks the root on the keyboard. The big knob of the key pad moves the voicing up and down; the performances (strum, arpeggio, pattern, harp) set the chord in motion. Everything is played live, nothing is programmed in steps.

The sound

Two engines, Sound and Bass, on sixteen voices at 48 kHz: virtual analog, FM and a physical-model reed piano, plus a sampler with 94 MB of free samples (pianos, harpsichords, pipe organ, recorders, ocarina, harmonicas, sax, harp, vibraphone, marimba, bells). In all, 232 sounds and 38 basses designed for Stame, from glass pads to acid lines, with two effects per engine, delay and reverb.

Rhythm and loops

The Beat has 64 patterns and 43 kits, sampled and synthesized. With DRUMS on, three knobs give density, groove and variation to drums that change while they play. The looper records four bars with a count-in, then overdubs, pauses and undoes. With AMBIENT, Stame almost plays by itself: slow chords in the key, the bass underneath and sparse notes, steered by the knobs and by every chord you play.

The screen

The Miyoo Flip is both screen and sound engine. The panel shows the chord, the tempo, the keyboard and the last control touched; the rest arrives when needed. There are six views and two themes, Day like a reflective LCD and Night like a fluorescent display, with type drawn for it. After twenty seconds without input the demo starts: six original pieces that show what the instrument can do.

The case

Keyboard, key pad and Flip are not modified: they rest in the console and come out in a minute. The console is 3D printed in PETG of three colours, in three trays joined by dovetails and M3 screws, without glue, and it comes apart. There is also a laser-cut version, in birch and engraved black acrylic.

Ready or as a kit

Stame is for sale assembled, or as a kit to build with the case instructions. Software, manual, quick reference and print projects are public on GitHub; the software installs and updates straight from the Flip, with Cirmolo, when a new version is out.
