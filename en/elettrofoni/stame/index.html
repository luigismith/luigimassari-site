Stame brings together in one case a keyboard, a pad of mechanical keys and a handheld console that acts as screen and sound engine: press one key and a full chord plays, with bass and rhythm.

The case is 3D printed in PETG of three colours, in trays that fit together without glue. Software, manual and case instructions are public on GitHub. Stame is for sale assembled, or as a kit to build with those instructions.

Specifications

Platform
Miyoo Flip handheld console
Control
Akai MPK mini IV MIDI keyboard and a fifteen-key macro pad
Sounds
232 sounds (181 synthesized, 51 sampled) and 38 basses; VA, FM, Reed Piano and sampler engines
Playing
Strum, arpeggio, pattern and harp; beat with 64 patterns and 43 kits; looper
Case
3D printed in PETG of three colours, in interlocking trays
Options
Ready to play, hand-assembled in Taranto, or as a kit with the instructions on GitHub
