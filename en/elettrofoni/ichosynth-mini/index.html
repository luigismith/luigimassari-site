Ichosynth Mini

Pocket step sequencer and sampler, made for training.

Price on request

Ichosynth Mini is a small instrument for step composing and playing samples: record them, trim them, sequence them. It's the instrument of the ICHOS — Suoni a margine training course, where participants built and played it.

Available assembled and tested, or as a kit with build documentation: a good fit for schools, training courses and anyone who wants to understand how a digital instrument comes to life.
