Eremo is a machine for sound environments: wood, silicon and quiet. Switch it on and it plays, with no menus and no presets to learn: it generates a soundscape that evolves by itself and always stays consonant, because harsh intervals are ruled out by design and the harmony rules are checked at every firmware build.

Ten atmospheres

Night, Dawn, Ocean, Cosmos, Fog, Ritual, Workshop, Desert, Ice and Forest. Each has a choir voice, a synthetic drum kit with three rhythmic textures and a sampled texture flowing under the drone.

Three ways to play it

In Ambient you let Eremo play. In Synth it becomes a polyphonic instrument with glide, played from a MIDI keyboard. In Maestro one finger plays a full chord, with extensions, jazz voicings, key lock, arpeggiator and strum.

Four knobs, two keys

Spheres picks the atmosphere, Light opens the filter and lights the sparks, Breath sets how fast the landscape changes, Sparks how many sparks fall. Every knob answers when turned, pushed and turned, or together with MODE. SHIFT/MODE changes mode and starts MIDI Learn; DRUMS/RHYTHM switches the drums on and sets their volume.

The sound

A five-voice wavetable drone with twelve timbres, a sampled sound and up to eight custom waves from the micro-SD; then a low-pass, band-pass or high-pass filter, chorus and flanger, a five-second tape echo and a double stereo reverb. The microphone captures a second and a half of sound that becomes a new choir voice; an optional thermal sensor makes the landscape «warm up».

Display and connections

The round display shows the wave like a phase-locked oscilloscope. Eremo speaks MIDI over USB with a computer and accepts a keyboard, with MIDI Learn on every control and external clock. The case is 3D printed, with a laser-engraved plywood panel and visible screws.
